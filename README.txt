TIFFIN LEDGER - BUILD YOUR ANDROID APK (free, about 5 minutes)

1. Create a free account at github.com and tap New repository (name: tiffin-ledger, Private is fine).
2. Tap "uploading an existing file" and drag EVERYTHING from this unzipped folder into it,
   including the www folder, package.json, capacitor.config.json, icon.png and the .github folder.
   (If the .github folder does not upload: tap Add file > Create new file, type
   .github/workflows/build-apk.yml as the name, paste the contents of build-apk.yml, and commit.)
3. Commit the files. Open the Actions tab. The "Build APK" job starts by itself (about 5 minutes).
4. When it shows a green tick, open it and download "Tiffin-Ledger-APK" at the bottom. Unzip it.
5. Send app-debug.apk to your phone (WhatsApp, Drive or USB) and open it.
   Android will ask you to allow "install unknown apps" for that app. Allow it, then Install.

Your data stays on the phone. Use Settings > Show my backup in the app regularly.
